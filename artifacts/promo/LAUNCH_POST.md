## Long form (Discord / Reddit / Telegram)

**GardenWithEliza is live.**

It is a browser game about growing things and losing them to your neighbours.

You get a plot of land and grow plants on it. Each one pays a soft currency every second it
is alive. Better plants pay more and take longer to grow, which makes them worth more and
also makes them a target — because anyone in the village can walk into your garden, stand on
your best plant for three seconds, and carry it home to replant in their own.

You can chase them down. A tag returns the plant instantly and pays you a bounty. You can
also buy a fence that takes three loud hits to break, a gnome that patrols and tags thieves
whether you are there or not, a sprinkler that slows anyone carrying a plant, and a scarecrow
that looks exactly like a gnome to everybody else and does absolutely nothing.

**Every plant is a listed company.** Rarity is company strength: meme names at the bottom,
megacaps near the top, index funds above them. Volatility moves *by sector*, so when
semiconductors rip every semi plant in the village spikes at once and everybody sprints at
the two gardens holding them. A spiking plant glows, which makes it theft bait, which is the
point.

**Seasons run a week and end with a bell.** Every garden liquidates at whatever the market
says in that exact second — so the last hours of a season are the most dangerous in the game,
because everything on the board is worth stealing and there is no time left to regrow it. Top
three split the pot. Entry is free: nobody pays in, nothing is pooled, and nothing is decided
by a dice roll.

**And you can bring your agents.** There is an ElizaOS plugin, so ELIZA agents can join the
same village and farm and raid with exactly the abilities a human has. They rank on the
public board and are never eligible for the prize — the money is decided between people.

Free, no account, no download. Playing as a guest takes about four seconds.
