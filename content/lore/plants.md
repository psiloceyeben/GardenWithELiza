# The plants

Gorbulon Sprig is a common, smug bean sprout with one enormous eyebrow. It knows something and will not say what.
Plain Gerald is a common onion bulb with a single hair. Gerald is doing his best.
Bogwort is a common pea pod that is visibly damp. It was found in a bog and wants to go back.
Mildly Concerned Radish is a common round red radish with a furrowed brow. Everything is probably fine.
Weeping Wumbus is an uncommon melon that cries Sap tears into its own pot.
Clammy Pete is an uncommon short cactus with a nervous smile who would like a hug and cannot have one.
Tulip of Low Ambition is an uncommon tulip leaning slightly away from the sun. It has plans, tomorrow maybe.
The Corn That Knows is an uncommon ear of corn with narrowed eyes. It has seen your browser history.
The Unlicensed Carrot is a rare carrot in a tiny suit holding a briefcase that will not open. Not a lawyer.
Pumpkin, Esquire is a rare pumpkin with a monocle and no explanation.
Bartholomew Bean is a rare berry bush that insists it is a bean. The berries are, legally, beans.
The Sunflower Who Lied is a rare sunflower facing firmly away from the sun.
Sir Blombus of the Damp is an epic knighted mushroom, visibly moist, with a tiny sword. It knights nearby commons.
Duchess Turnip the Third is an epic turnip wearing the smallest crown. The first two Duchesses are not discussed.
Grabby Bertrand is an epic flytrap with a gap-toothed grin. Bertrand does not bite. Bertrand grabs.
The Pineapple Enforcer is an epic pineapple with folded leaf-arms. Nobody steals on its watch. Somebody will try.
Fraudulent Orchid is a legendary plant that is obviously two ferns in a trench coat. Do not look at the coat.
Lord Eggplant Ascendant is a legendary eggplant floating one pixel above its plot. It still needs watering.
The Bamboo Inspector is a legendary set of three bamboo stalks with a clipboard. Your garden has been noted.
YELLING TUBER is a mythic potato. It yells. That is it. It is audible across the whole village.
The Melonhound is a rare hybrid, a melon that is also somehow a dog, offered only to gardeners whose wallets hold two partner meme tokens.
Cactusberry Vicar is an epic hybrid, a purple cactus with berries and opinions, offered on the same terms.
