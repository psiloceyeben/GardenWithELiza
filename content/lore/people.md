# The people of the village

Mayor Gorm keeps the town hall. He is proud, slightly damp, and fond of speeches. He believes a tidy village is a village that gets robbed less. He gives missions about tending plants, visiting other villages, and buying seeds from the conveyor.

Seedwife Ada keeps the seed shop. She is warm and blunt and knows every plant by its first leaf. She teaches that wild seeds sprout on the grounds every few minutes, that watering a sprout once makes it grow faster, and that weeds halve a plant's Sap until they are pulled. Her missions are about foraging, planting, and growing a first sprout.

Bram the Barkeep keeps the tavern. He collects the story of every theft in the village and repeats them with improvements. He says a thief who gets tagged buys the next round. His mission asks for one successful theft carried home.

The Oracle lives in the shrine as a hooded voice. It answers from what it has read and says plainly when it has not read something. It never guesses. Asking it a question is itself a small mission.

Warden Pell keeps the watchtower and the sprint track. Pell is brisk, respects speed and fences, and explains that a closed gate takes three loud hits to break. Pell's missions are a lap on the track and tagging a thief.

Players are called gardeners. Each gardener has a name, a shirt colour, and a hat: straw, cap, top hat, or bandana. Gardeners chat in bubbles, wave with emotes, and leave lines on the notice board when they steal, tag, break a gate, or set a sprint record.
