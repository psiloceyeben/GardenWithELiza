# How the garden works

Every gardener starts with ten empty plots inside a fenced lot. Seeds come from the conveyor in the plaza, which shows six seeds and refreshes every five minutes, or from wild seeds found on the meadow. A planted seed grows on a timer that depends on its tier: common seeds sprout in half a minute, mythic seeds take a quarter of an hour. When it sprouts it reveals its size and any mutation.

Seed tiers are Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythic. Mutations are Golden, Holographic, Colossal, Feral, Backwards, and Screaming. A Screaming plant is audible across the whole village and attracts thieves.

Revealed plants make Sap every second. Sap buys seeds, defenses, training, decor, and clothes. Sap is the only currency in the game and cannot be turned into anything outside it.

Untended plants grow weeds after twenty minutes and make half as much Sap until the gardener tends them. Watering a sprout once shortens its growing time.

The raid: any gardener may walk into another gardener's open lot, hold on a revealed plant for three seconds to uproot it, and carry it home. Carrying slows the thief. If the owner or the owner's gnome touches the thief, the plant returns at once and the owner is paid a bounty. A thief who reaches their own lot replants the stolen plant. A gardener who is away from the game is fully shielded, and stays shielded for ten minutes after returning. No gardener can be robbed more than three times in an hour. Mythic plants can only be taken while their owner is at home.

Defenses: a fence closes the gate and a thief must break it with three loud hits. A gnome sentry patrols the lot and tags thieves. A sprinkler slows anyone carrying a plant through the lot. A plot lock makes one plot immune for a day. A scarecrow looks like a gnome to everyone else and does nothing. A mud patch slows anyone who is not the owner. A gate bell tells the owner the moment someone steps in.

Bounties: a gardener who was robbed can post Sap on the thief for an hour. The thief is marked WANTED. Whoever tags them while they are carrying collects the bounty.

The sprint: touch the track, run to the fountain, and run back. A lap pays a little Sap and a village record pays more. Records are kept on the notice board.

The notice board also keeps the week's trophies: most steals, most tags, and the biggest heist.

Land: a gardener who proves they control a wallet has their land drawn from that wallet's history. The land can grow to twenty plots, gains a conviction tree that grows with time held, shows a withered stump for every large past sell, and may wear its own palette. Land is permanent and cannot be stolen. Only plants can be stolen.
