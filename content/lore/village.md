# The village

A village in Pons Garden is a walled square of meadow with a plaza at its heart and sixteen fenced garden lots around it. Roads ring the plaza and run out to the four sides. Ponds sit in the corners. A short street of buildings stands along the north wall: the town hall, the seed shop, the tavern, the shrine, and the watchtower. Each building has a keeper who stands at its door.

The plaza holds a fountain, benches, lamps, a notice board, the seed conveyor stall, and the sprint track. The signpost at the south edge of the plaza leads to other villages. A visitor arrives on the plaza and may walk anywhere, raid any open garden, and carry a stolen plant home through the signpost.

Every village wears one of eight palettes: Verdant Rows, Molten Meadow, Static Bog, Sugar Hollow, Dusk Flats, Frost Ridge, Ash Yard, and Neon Marsh. The palette changes nothing but the colours.

The village keeps a shared clock. Every twenty minutes an event runs for three minutes: Seed Rain drops wild seeds across the plaza, Screaming Hour makes every plant yell, and Golden Hour gives fresh sprouts a second chance at a mutation.

Wild seeds sprout on the meadow outside the lots. There are never more than eight at once, and each lasts ten minutes. Anyone may pick one up for free. Rare wild seeds are announced on the notice board.
